About

This is a web-based system that facilitates monitoring of emergencies around WASH (Water, Sanitation and Health)

Background

Introduction

COVID-19 is a global pandemic affecting most countries and territories around the world. As of 14th April 2020, there were 1.9 million confirmed COVID-19 cases resulting in close to 120,000 deaths with the first case of COVID-19 being confirmed in Kenya on 13 March 2020. UNICEF and Partners came together to address issues related to the Pandemic, including but not limited to ensuring the provision and continuity of WASH services.

Project

To enhance coordination and accountability at national and county level, UNICEF, Ministry of Water and the Ministry of Health with the support of Sweden Embassy partnered to conceptualize a Covid-19 Response Platform.
However as time passed, the need for a more versatile tool that handles All WASH emergencies was conceived.

System

AGS (Advanced Geospatial Solutions) was then contracted to design and implement the dynamic web based data portal with the capacity to monitor any emerging emergency situation under WASH. The data portal allows its respective users to :-

  • Monitor emergencies at national and county levels.
  • Monitor partner participation in emergencies and the activities they are undertaking
  • View generated reports based on the 5W Matrix
  • Download reports
  • Dynamically configure data such as indicators.
  • View reports on your computer and mobile device.
